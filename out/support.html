Support

Quick answers for your next shift.

Lives booster

Lives adds one heart when a shift has room for it. It stays inactive while all three hearts are full.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in Tiny Warehouse and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Daily Shift reminders

Notifications are optional. You can enable or disable them from the game’s Settings screen or iOS Settings.

Starter Pack

The Starter Pack is an optional one-time purchase. If a purchase is pending, the game will grant it after Apple confirms the transaction.
